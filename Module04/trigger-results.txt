## UPDATE and UPSERT side effects
alarms after below-to-above UPSERT: 0
audits after below-to-above UPSERT: 1
audits after baseline no-op UPSERT: 2
audits after conditional no-op UPSERT: 2

The required alarm trigger is INSERT-only. An UPSERT that follows its UPDATE
branch can therefore cross the vibration threshold without creating an alarm.
The assigned audit trigger records any UPDATE OF value, even a no-op; the
conditional UPSERT in ingest.py avoids that redundant update.
